## Figure semantics

The publisher caption identifies panel **b** as a stacked bar plot of the top ten relative abundances of methanogenic archaeal genera in the MGS dataset at time point 7, grouped by center. Categories are G (Graz), K (Klagenfurt), and L (Leoben). The official Source Data PDF maps Figure 1b to the retained count table, metadata, and archaeal taxonomy files and notes Microbiome Explorer CSS normalization.
